# First-user pilot plan

This is a founder-operated, zero-spend 30-day pilot proposal. Its purpose is to learn whether a small, well-supported service area can produce suitable requests and completed jobs. Targets below are goals for the pilot, not current results or promises of demand, earnings, coverage, response time, vetting, or safety.

## Pilot choice and entry gates

Proposed starting area: **Mosta**, as a practical single-locality assumption only. There is no research here proving Mosta has the strongest demand or worker supply. Confirm it through worker conversations before committing. If fewer than five suitable workers in the chosen categories want to serve Mosta, select one other locality based on those conversations and document the choice. Do not open Malta-wide promotion during this pilot.

Start with **home cleaning** and **furniture assembly/setup**, limited to ordinary, non-hazardous tasks. Ask workers to exclude work they are not trained or equipped to do, including hazardous cleaning, power-tool operation in customer-participation modes, heavy lifting, and electrical, plumbing, gas, structural, or other regulated work. The app catalog includes regulated categories; do not promote them as part of this pilot.

The staging site at <https://daqqa-t-id-staging.daqqatid-75d7e2e4.workers.dev> is a fictional, noindex demo. Its profiles and bookings are test records, its booking payments are simulated, and the public HTTPS lifecycle/notification/private-photo smoke has passed. Real device alert display still needs a phone test. It is suitable only for a clearly labelled demo invitation. It is not a live marketplace or a destination for real worker/customer details.

Before real-pilot invitations, the owner/operator should complete and record all of these launch checks:

- The final pilot URL and production data store are configured separately from staging; public pages, registration, cookie/CSRF writes, image privacy, account recovery/support path, and the full booking lifecycle work over HTTPS.
- A real booking has been exercised end to end with consenting test participants, and the operator can see and respond to new requests. Test the bell inbox and opted-in phone alerts with each helper; assign a dashboard check cadence as a fallback and explain it during onboarding.
- Real payment processing is either deliberately out of scope and plainly explained before a request, or has separately completed provider, accounting, legal, and refund checks. The current app uses simulated payments; do not ask for card details or describe a simulation as a charge.
- Privacy notice, terms, support contact, incident/escalation route, cancellation/refund wording, and worker consent for public profile material have been reviewed for this real pilot. These are review gates, not claims that the present placeholder text is ready.
- The operator has a simple record of worker consent, service scope, locality, current availability, and profile corrections/removal requests. Do not expose private identity or verification documents in public copy.

Do not begin customer promotion until **10 real workers have consented to participate**, have completed a profile suitable for the pilot, and the launch checks above pass. Consent means they understand this is an early, manually supported pilot; agree to the listed public information and pilot follow-up; have reviewed their own profile; and can pause or leave. Any required verification must be reviewed through the normal process before the relevant service is offered. Ten is a recruiting goal and a promotion gate, not a claim that ten workers exist today.

## 30-day operating schedule

Keep one simple weekly tally with counts and date ranges. Exclude demo/test data. Count unique people, not messages; retain aggregate counts and only the minimum contact/follow-up notes needed to run the pilot.

| Days | Work | Goal by the end of the phase |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Choose Mosta provisionally; confirm available categories and the exclusions above; prepare a one-page explanation and consent checklist; test the operator’s dashboard workflow. | Written scope, named operator, support/escalation route, and a working onboarding appointment flow. |
| 4–10 | Invite workers individually through existing relationships or referrals. Ask permission before sharing a group post. Hold short assisted setup sessions. | 15 relevant worker conversations, with 10 consenting worker participants as the stretch target. Record declines as counts/reasons only where volunteered. |
| 11–14 | Help participants complete and review their own profiles, hours, locality/radius, categories, price model, and public details. Complete any required verification review. Ask each worker to confirm what requests they can accept and how often the operator can check. | 10 consenting participants with reviewed, usable pilot profiles; at least 6 currently able to take suitable enquiries. If fewer than 10 consent, keep recruiting and do not promote to customers. |
| 15–21 | After the entry gates pass, invite a small customer cohort through permitted local channels. Start with up to 20 invitations; give a human contact route for questions. Check new requests at the stated cadence and pause invitations if capacity is exceeded. | Goal: 5 valid requests, 3 accepted/confirmed jobs, and a recorded outcome for every request. These are learning targets, not expected demand. |
| 22–30 | Continue only if workers can respond and the operator can support the flow. Follow up with each participant once, ask for feedback without requesting public reviews, and review drop-offs and safety concerns. | Goal: 10 valid requests cumulatively, 6 accepted/confirmed jobs, and 4 completed jobs if timing permits. No pressure to complete a job to meet a target. |

Assisted setup should take about 20–30 minutes as a planning estimate. Let the worker create and control their account, enter their own password privately, review profile text and prices, choose the categories/modes they actually offer, set realistic hours and travel range, and submit any required verification information themselves. The operator may explain fields or type text the worker dictates while they are present, but must never request, see, or enter a password, claim self-reported details were verified, or approve evidence without review. Get explicit consent before publishing a photo, biography, or portfolio image. Give each worker a simple way to request a correction or removal.

## Weekly measures and decisions

Review counts each week, alongside a short note on what slowed people down. Suggested pilot funnel goals are cumulative by day 30; denominators make the results interpretable:

| Measure | Day-30 goal | Definition |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Relevant worker conversations | 15 | Distinct eligible workers who heard the pilot explanation. |
| Consenting workers | 10 | Distinct workers who consented to pilot participation and public profile details. Promotion gate. |
| Usable profiles | 10 | Consenting profiles reviewed by their owners, with pilot service/locality, price choice, and contact/availability expectations clear. Required verification is complete where applicable. |
| Customer invitations | Up to 20 initially | Distinct local people invited after launch gates; do not count broad post views as invitations. |
| Valid requests | 10 | A request in the pilot area/category with enough detail to decide whether it is suitable. Exclude demo, spam, duplicates, and out-of-scope tasks. |
| Accepted/confirmed bookings | 6 | Count each booking once at the relevant status; report the actual status definitions separately. |
| Completed jobs | 4 if timing permits | Only jobs both parties report completed; do not infer completion from payment records. |
| Safety/privacy incidents | 0 target | Record and respond to every report; a zero target must never discourage reporting. |

For each week, record: worker conversations, consented participants, complete usable profiles, required verification submissions/decisions, customer invitations, valid requests, worker response count and time band, accepted/confirmed/completed/cancelled requests, cancellations and volunteered reasons, support contacts, incidents, and review requests/submissions. Use aggregate counts; do not put addresses, identity documents, passwords, payment information, or unnecessary sensitive job details in a spreadsheet. Keep demo data out of these figures. Do not claim conversion rates from a tiny or changing cohort without showing both the numerator and denominator.

**Pause new customer invitations** if fewer than six workers are currently able to serve the advertised scope, suitable requests go unanswered, the assigned operator cannot keep the stated check cadence, a serious safety/privacy issue is unresolved, or the app/payment wording could mislead someone. Contact affected participants through the agreed channel, resolve the issue, and resume only when service capacity and support are clear.

**Expand only after review:** consider a second locality or a third category after at least four weeks of operation with ten consenting workers, at least six completed jobs, no unresolved serious incident, at least 80% of valid requests receiving a worker response within the cadence promised to participants, and the operator able to support the workload. These are proposed decision thresholds, not market facts. Add one locality or category at a time, recheck supply and exclusions, then measure another cohort. Do not infer nationwide coverage from a successful locality pilot.

## Zero-spend acquisition and onboarding

Use worker referrals and permission-based local groups first. Share one invitation at a time, explain the early-stage limits, and invite a short conversation rather than claiming there are customers waiting. Ask a group administrator first, follow their rules, and post only after permission. Do not scrape group members, add people to lists, send bulk unsolicited messages, buy ads, or post repeatedly. The scripts and printable link/QR copy are in [LAUNCH-KIT.md](LAUNCH-KIT.md).

Use two different destinations:

- **Demo now:** the staging URL above, sent only with the explicit fictional-demo wording in the launch kit. No real personal, worker, customer, payment, or verification data should be entered there.
- **Real pilot later:** replace `{{LIVE_PILOT_URL}}` in the kit only after the entry gates pass and the actual public URL has been checked. Keep the placeholder until then; do not redirect it to staging.

For a real pilot, invite customers only after the worker cohort and launch checks pass. Start with known local networks and permitted community groups, cap invitations according to available worker capacity, and stop when requests outpace the operator’s ability to support them. A worker may share their own profile link only after reviewing it and consenting to its public details; the link is not a booking promise.

## Optional support from the first real launch

The owner selected low-pressure, value-based monetization. The implemented first model is an **optional, one-off platform support payment** after a completed booking, also reachable from the footer. Browsing, joining, booking, search ranking and response priority do not depend on paying. There are no subscriptions or invented premium benefits. Lead with the useful information and coordination the app actually provides, then offer the choice once it has helped.

[MONETIZATION.md](MONETIZATION.md) explains how to connect an owner-controlled Stripe Payment Link in Production. Staging always disables it; no real checkout is connected today. Stripe shows the chosen amount and terms and provides payment records. A link click or return to the app is never counted as revenue. The simulated booking commission ledger remains separate and does not collect money.

For the initial consenting real cohort, record completed jobs, repeat use, optional-support page views only if consented analytics are configured, actual paid support payments from Stripe, provider fees, refunds and support time. Do not infer willingness to pay from clicks. Review whether the service saved effort and whether voluntary revenue covers its costs. Introduce no additional fee or recurring plan without a concrete benefit and clear advance terms.

The illustrative math in [LAUNCH-KIT.md](LAUNCH-KIT.md) is scenario analysis only. Actual demand, prices, costs, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, and margins are unknown. No income is guaranteed.
