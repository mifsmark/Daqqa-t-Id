## Where to promote, in order

1. **Known local helpers and referrals:** start conversations and learn what would make the profile useful. Recruit both launch categories before promising coverage.
2. **Mosta/community Facebook groups:** use one relevant, administrator-approved post inviting helpers first and customers after readiness. Respond to interested people through the agreed channel.
3. **WhatsApp Status and existing contacts:** show a short demo today; share the real service or a consenting helper’s profile after launch.
4. **Short Instagram/Facebook videos:** demonstrate an actual task — finding a helper, requesting a time, receiving an update. Label demo footage. Use subtitles and one clear link.
5. **Local shops and noticeboards:** with permission, use a simple card linking to the verified production service. Generate the QR only when the final URL is known.
6. **Search:** use accurate service pages and real profiles, clear locality/service descriptions and original photos shared with consent. Let useful, distinct pages earn search traffic; avoid duplicate locality pages and fabricated reviews.

This ordering is a low-cost starting experiment, not a verified ranking of acquisition channels. Measure which source produces completed jobs and repeat use. Paid advertising can wait until suitable requests receive reliable responses.

## A simple sharing loop

After a successfully completed job, invite the customer to leave an honest review and optionally share the helper’s public profile with someone who might find it useful. The app already supports profile sharing/WhatsApp links. Ask the helper to share their own profile with permission. Use real results and consented photos; never post a customer’s address or private booking details.

**Optional review/share message**

> Thanks for trying Daqqa t’Id. Was the helper useful? An honest review helps the next person choose. If someone you know needs the same service, you can share the helper’s public profile — only if you’d like to.

**Optional support wording after value was delivered**

> Was Daqqa t’Id useful? You can choose a one-off amount to support the platform. It’s optional, separate from your helper’s price, and you can keep using the service without paying.

Use the support invitation only after the real production checkout is connected and checked. A link click is not a payment; reconcile actual receipts in Stripe. No sharing or payment is required to use the service.
